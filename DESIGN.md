# Folio design

Cursor-inspired restrained utility. Neutral chrome, quiet hairline separators, readable Chinese system fonts, muted blue for selection. Desktop fills the window, compact left sidebar, central table and reading tabs, optional right annotation panel. Article content is the visual center. Tables use alignment, not tile containers. No decorative gradients, glows, hero art, KPI dashboard, or staged entrance motion.

Controls 32px, rows 44px, sidebar about 200px, reader max-width 760px, prose 18px with 1.8 line-height. Keyboard focus must remain visible. Empty, loading, error, disconnected and no-key states remain usable and honest. Existing palette adapts to system dark mode.
